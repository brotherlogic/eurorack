This setup focuses on a self-playing "West Coast" style, utilizing a generative 'brain' and resonator alongside the Cre8audio NiftyCASE bundled modules, filling exactly 84HP.

## Layout Rationale
1. **Cellz:** Hands-on touch controller on the far left for easy access.
2. **Pam's:** Sits next to Cellz to provide the master clock and distribute modulation.
3. **Dice:** Takes clocking from Pam's to generate organic melodies and rhythms.
4. **Chipz:** First sound source, taking pitch sequences from Cellz or Dice.
5. **Resonate:** Second sound source (physical modeling). Kept adjacent to Chipz for easy patching into effects.
6. **Mojave:** Sits right after the sound sources to process their audio into granular clouds.
7. **3x MIA:** At the far right to mix the outputs before sending the final mix out.
