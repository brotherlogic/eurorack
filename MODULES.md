## Recommendation & Rationale
If you want the most 'hands-on' control and a self-playing system, **Option B** with **Mojave** is fantastic. Mojave has a built-in scale quantizer which ensures granular clouds stay in key with your melodies.

With 60HP, Option B becomes powerful because Marbles (Dice) can take over the sequencing in an 'organic' way, creating a truly self-playing ambient system.
