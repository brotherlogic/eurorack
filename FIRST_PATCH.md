# First Patch: The Generative Cloud

This guide helps you set up your first self-playing ambient patch using your current modules (excluding Pam's Pro Workout).

## 1. The Brain (ALA Dice)
*   **Action:** Ensure no external clock is connected. Turn the **Rate** knob until the lights blink at a steady "heartbeat" pace.
*   **Patch:** Connect **Dice X1** (bottom-row CV output) $\rightarrow$ **ALA Resonate V/Oct**.
*   **Patch:** Connect **Dice t1** (top-row Gate output) $\rightarrow$ **ALA Resonate Strum**.
*   *Result:* Dice is now playing random melodies on Resonate.

## 2. The Sound Source (ALA Resonate)
*   **Settings:** Set **Structure** and **Brightness** knobs to 12 o'clock.
*   **Patch:** Connect **Resonate Out** $\rightarrow$ **Mojave L** (Audio input).

## 3. The Texture (Qu-bit Mojave)
*   **Settings:** Turn **Structure** to 10 o'clock and **Depth** to 2 o'clock.
*   **Patch:** Connect **Mojave Out L** $\rightarrow$ **3x MIA Section 1, Jack A** (top-left).
*   **Patch:** Connect **Mojave Out R** $\rightarrow$ **3x MIA Section 2, Jack A** (middle-left).

## 4. The Manual Drone (Cellz & Chipz)
*   **Patch:** Connect **Cellz CV 1** $\rightarrow$ **Chipz OSC 1 V/Oct**.
*   **Patch:** Connect **Chipz OSC 1 Out** $\rightarrow$ **3x MIA Section 3, Jack A** (bottom-left).
*   *Result:* Touch the pads on Cellz to change the pitch of the Chipz drone.

## 5. The Final Mix (3x MIA & NiftyCASE)
*   **Setup:** Leave the outputs for Section 1 and Section 2 **empty**. This "cascades" the signal down.
*   **Patch:** Connect **3x MIA Section 3 Output** (bottom-right jack) $\rightarrow$ **NiftyCASE Audio Out**.
*   **Volume Controls (MIA Knobs):**
    *   **Top Knob:** Right = Mojave Left volume.
    *   **Middle Knob:** Right = Mojave Right volume.
    *   **Bottom Knob:** Right = Chipz Drone volume.

---

### Performance Tips
*   **Morph the Clouds:** Slowly turn Mojave's **Structure** or **Whirl** knobs.
*   **Change the Melody:** Turn Dice's **Chance** (left for steady, right for chaos) or **Range** knobs.
*   **Play the Bass:** Slide your fingers across the **Cellz** pads to shift the foundation of the track.
