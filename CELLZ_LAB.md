# Module Lab: Learning Cellz

This guide is designed to help you master the core mechanics of **Cellz** by treating it as a manual keyboard before using it as a sequencer.

## 1. The "Lab" Setup
*   Connect **Cellz CV 1** $\rightarrow$ **Chip 1 "Filter" jack** (on Chipz).
*   Connect **Chip 1 Out** $\rightarrow$ **3x MIA Section 1, Jack A**.
*   Connect **3x MIA Section 3 Output** $\rightarrow$ **NiftyCASE Output**.
*   *Result:* You should hear a constant tone from Chipz.

## 2. Exercise 1: Programming the Grid
Each of the 16 pads is a "storage slot" for a specific pitch.
1.  **Touch Pad 1** (Top Left). It will light up.
2.  Turn the **Left Knob** on Cellz. You will hear the pitch of Chipz change. Set it to a low "bass" note.
3.  **Touch Pad 2** (Top Row, second from left).
4.  Turn the **Left Knob** again to set this to a higher "lead" note.
5.  *The Lesson:* You have now "stored" two notes. You can jump between these two pads to play them like a keyboard.

## 3. Exercise 2: The "Dual Brain"
Cellz has two independent outputs. Every pad stores **two** notes at once.
1.  Connect **Cellz CV 2** $\rightarrow$ **Chip 2 "Width" jack** (on Chipz).
2.  Connect **Chip 2 Out** $\rightarrow$ **3x MIA Section 1, Jack B**. (Turn the bottom dial on MIA Section 1 to hear it).
3.  **Touch Pad 1** again.
4.  Turn the **Left Knob** to change the pitch of Chip 1.
5.  Turn the **Right Knob** to change the pitch of Chip 2.
6.  *The Lesson:* Every pad on the grid is a "preset" that can change the pitch of two different oscillators simultaneously.

## 4. Exercise 3: Performance Modes
Look at the buttons at the very top of the module:
*   **Gate Mode:** Toggle the button to change if a pad stays "on" after you let go (Latch) or turns "off" when you release (Momentary).
*   **The Arrows:** If you plug a clock (like from Dice) into the **Horizontal ($\rightarrow$)** or **Vertical ($\downarrow$)** jacks, Cellz will "auto-play" through the pads you've programmed.

---

### Key Takeaways
*   **Left Knob** = Controls CV 1.
*   **Right Knob** = Controls CV 2.
*   **Arrows** = "Invisible fingers" that move the sequencer when they receive a pulse.
